Privacy Policy - Snipt

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Version: 1.0

Overview

Welcome to Snipt (“the App”). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how (little) data is handled when you use the App.

Our Core Principle: All silence detection and video processing happens entirely on your device using on-device AI (Silero VAD) and Android MediaCodec. We do not collect, access, store, or transmit any of your videos, audio, detected silences, processed output, or personal content to any external server, and we do not have the ability to view anything you process using the App.

Data Controller: This policy is provided by SSManha Ltd, a company registered in the United Kingdom. Contact: support@ssmanha.com

Who Can Use the App

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children, as the App does not collect data at all (see below). If you believe a child under 13 has provided us personal information directly (e.g., via email contact), please contact us and we will delete it.

Data We Collect

We do not collect any of the following, ever: - Your video files or any portion of them - Audio tracks, detected silence segments, or processed output videos - Camera images, microphone recordings, or any media content - Personal information (name, email, address, etc.), except what you voluntarily provide by emailing us directly - Device identifiers or advertising IDs - Usage analytics, tracking data, or behavioural profiling - Location information - Contacts, social media, or account data - Cookies or tracking technologies of any kind (the App is a native mobile app and does not use cookies)

Data Handled by Google Play Services (outside our control): The following data may be collected by Google Play Services on Google’s behalf, governed entirely by Google’s Privacy Policy:

  • Purchase history (for subscription/Pro feature verification via Google Play Billing)
  • Anonymised app usage statistics
  • Crash reports (if enabled by you)
  • Device information (for compatibility purposes)

This data is transmitted directly between your device and Google, not through us. Where Google processes this data outside the UK/EEA, such transfers are governed by Google’s own safeguards (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses), as detailed in Google’s Privacy Policy.

Legal Basis for Processing (UK GDPR)

Because we do not collect personal data through the App itself, our processing footprint is minimal. To the extent any data is processed (e.g., Google Play Billing verifying your subscription, or you emailing us directly), our legal basis is:

  • Performance of a contract: for subscription verification necessary to provide the Pro features you purchased.
  • Legitimate interests: for responding to support or contact emails you send us voluntarily.

Data Storage & Security

  • All video processing, silence detection, and output generation occurs entirely on your device.
  • Temporary cache files created during processing are managed by the Android OS and are never transmitted.
  • No cloud sync or server uploads occur at any point.
  • You have full control: clearing the App’s data or uninstalling the App removes all local cache and preferences.

Retention: Temporary files and cache are managed by the Android system. Local app data and preferences are deleted automatically when you clear the App’s data or uninstall it.

App Permissions

  • Storage / Media Access (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE and WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE where required): To allow you to select video files for processing and to save the resulting silence-removed videos to your device. All reading and writing happens locally.
  • Internet: Required solely so Google Play Billing can verify subscription/Pro purchases. No app feature uses this permission to transmit videos, audio, detected silences, or any personal content.

Processing Details

The App processes one video at a time. Silence detection is performed on-device using Silero VAD. Video encoding/decoding uses Android MediaCodec. The original video, detected silence segments, and the final output remain exclusively on your device. You retain full control over which detected silences to keep or remove.

Third-Party Libraries

The App uses the following third-party libraries, which operate locally on-device or strictly under their own respective policies: Silero VAD (on-device voice activity detection), Android MediaCodec, Google Play Billing.

Data Breach Notification

In the unlikely event that a data breach occurs that affects any personal information we hold (e.g., data you’ve emailed us), we will notify affected users and, where legally required, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), without undue delay and in line with UK GDPR requirements.

Your Rights (UK GDPR)

Where we hold any personal data about you (e.g., emails you’ve sent us), you have the right to:

  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data;
  • Object to or restrict our processing;
  • Request data portability, where applicable;
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you believe your data protection rights have been violated.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@ssmanha.com. We will respond within one month, as required by UK GDPR.

Children’s Privacy

The App is designed for general audiences aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has contacted us directly with personal data, please reach out and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified in-app. Continued use of the App after such changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
